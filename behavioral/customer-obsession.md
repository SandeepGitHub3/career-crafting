# Customer Obsession Stories

---

## Story 1: Deliveroo – Partner Billing Automation (Subscriptions)

### Situation
- The operations team spent significant time manually generating monthly billing for ~2,000 partners in the HK market.
- Process was slow, error-prone, and limited scalability to new regions.
- Delayed billing caused frustration among partners and high manual workload for internal teams.

### Task
- Automate the **end-of-month partner billing process** to:
  - Eliminate manual effort and RPA dependency.
  - Improve billing accuracy and turnaround time.
  - Enable expansion to multiple markets with minimal ops effort.

### Action
- Collaborated with **operations and finance teams** to understand workflow pain points.
- Designed a **subscription-based billing automation** using **AWS Lambda + Step Functions** for scalability and reliability.
- Broke down tasks, mentored engineers, and ensured timely delivery.
- Built monitoring dashboards for transparency and issue tracking.
- Collected feedback from partner-facing teams to improve exception handling.

### Result
- Reduced manual billing effort by **~90%**; processing time dropped from days to a few hours per market.
- Enabled expansion to new regions without increasing headcount.
- Improved billing accuracy and reduced partner escalations.
- Recognized internally as a “game-changer” for partner operations.

### Key Takeaway
- Demonstrated customer obsession by **starting from the partner pain point**.
- Built a scalable, reliable solution that improved both **partner experience** and **internal efficiency**.

## Story 2: Deliveroo – Restaurant Churn & Reactivation Workflow

### Situation
- The partner churn/reactivation process lacked structure and visibility.
- Manual processes created data inconsistencies and accountability issues for AEs and partners.
- Partners faced delays and confusion during reactivation.

### Task
- Design a **streamlined backend workflow** for churning and reactivating partners.
- Improve transparency, auditability, and speed of operations.

### Action
- Collaborated with **Account Executives, Salesforce, and Data teams** to map pain points.
- Built a **unified workflow and APIs** with audit trails for all partner actions.
- Defined reactivation conditions to minimize back-and-forth between AEs and ops.
- Mentored engineers and led task breakdown and execution.

### Result
- Reduced churn-to-reactivation time by **~40%**.
- Improved visibility and accountability across teams.
- Boosted partner satisfaction and retention.
- Workflow adopted as a reference for other markets.

### Key Takeaway
- Customer obsession shown by **focusing on partner transparency and responsiveness**.
- Delivered a process that increased trust and efficiency for both partners and internal teams.

## Story 3: UpGrad – Real-Time Leaderboard for Learner Engagement

### Situation
- Learners were losing motivation mid-course, leading to drop-offs.
- Product team hypothesized that gamification could improve engagement.

### Task
- Build a **real-time leaderboard system** to motivate learners through transparency and healthy competition.
- Ensure scalability across multiple cohorts and responsiveness at scale.

### Action
- Collaborated with **product and learner success teams** to understand user psychology.
- Designed a **Kafka Streams + Redis** architecture to handle **250–1000 learners per cohort** and scale to numerous cohorts simultaneously.
- Built APIs providing instant progress updates and rankings.
- Collected post-launch feedback to refine visibility and scoring logic.

### Result
- Achieved **sub-second updates** and **zero latency complaints**.
- Boosted learner activity and time-on-platform by **15–20%**.
- Became a core engagement feature used in future UpGrad products.

### Key Takeaway
- Demonstrated customer obsession by **focusing on learner motivation** rather than just feature delivery.
- Delivered a technically scalable system with measurable user impact.

## Quick Pointers for Interview Delivery
- Start each story with **“The customer problem was…”**
- Emphasize **listening to customer pain**, not just implementing features.
- End with **quantified impact** and what you learned.
- Mention collaboration across functions (Ops, Product, Finance, AEs).
- Keep it to **2 minutes per story** for behavioral interviews.
