# Ownership Story 

---
## 1. Improving Service Operational Excellence Score (Deliveroo)

### Situation
- Deliveroo had an **Engineering Operational Excellence Scorecard** that evaluated each service across metrics like:
  - Dependency & version upgrades  
  - Open vulnerabilities  
  - Security issues  
  - Test coverage  
  - Build reliability and monitoring health  
- One of the core services I worked on consistently scored **around 64**, ranking among the lower-performing services in the org.
- Although everyone was aware of the low score, ownership was unclear — teams focused on feature delivery, and reliability improvements kept getting postponed.

---

### Task
- Take **ownership of improving the service’s operational excellence score** from 64 upwards.
- Identify gaps across all scorecard metrics, prioritize work, and drive the changes.
- Balance improvements with ongoing feature delivery without disrupting timelines.

---

### Action
- Reviewed the **scorecard framework** in detail to understand the contribution of each metric.  
- Created a **focused improvement plan**, breaking down tasks across:
  - **Version upgrades** (frameworks, dependencies, libraries)  
  - **Fixing open vulnerabilities** and improving security posture  
  - **Increasing code coverage** through unit and integration test additions  
  - **Improving build and deployment reliability**  
- Logged and tracked each improvement in Jira, ensuring visibility across sprints.  
- Collaborated with platform, security, and QA teams to validate fixes and get alignment.  
- Set up **bi-weekly progress reviews** to track score improvements and share updates with engineering leadership.  
- Encouraged peers to fix long-standing items proactively by showing visible progress in scorecard improvements.  

---

### Result
- Improved the service’s score **from 64 → 85** within two quarters.  
- Achieved 100% on dependency and security-related metrics.  
- Increased code coverage by **~15%**, reducing regression issues.  
- Build reliability improved significantly — failed builds reduced by **~50%**.  
- The success of this initiative motivated other engineers to start working on their own service scorecards.  
- Recognized by my manager for **taking full ownership of reliability and quality metrics**, not just feature work.

---

### Key Talking Points
- “Ownership means caring about what others overlook.”  
- “I didn’t wait for direction — I took initiative to make reliability measurable.”  
- “By driving score improvements, I built credibility and helped influence a culture of excellence.”
